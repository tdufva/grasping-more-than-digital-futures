About the Collective

Grasping More-than-Digital Futures is an informal interdisciplinary research collective for scholars exploring the intersections of art, craft, sloyd, education, technology, culture, and digital transformation.

Our work engages with post-digital education, post-AI pedagogies, sustainable computing, digital craft, embodiment, feminist data sciences, critical artistic research, and more-than-digital ways of knowing and making.

Research Themes

  • Post-digital education and art education
  • Post-AI pedagogies and critical AI studies
  • Craft, sloyd, digital materiality, and making
  • Sustainable computing and ecological technologies
  • Embodiment, feminist data sciences, and experimental practice

What We Are Grasping Toward

After Digital Promises

We ask what education, art, and culture become when digital tools are ordinary, uneven, material, and politically charged.

Crafted Knowledge

We treat craft, sloyd, making, touch, repair, and situated practice as serious ways of thinking with technology.

Critical Technologies

We study AI, data, platforms, infrastructures, and sustainable computing through feminist, ecological, and cultural lenses.

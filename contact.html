Contact / Join Us

Researchers interested in more-than-digital questions are warmly invited to get in touch. The group is informal, collaborative, and open to interdisciplinary exchange.

Invitation

We welcome doctoral researchers, postdoctoral researchers, university lecturers, professors, independent scholars, artists, educators, and other researchers working across art, craft, sloyd, education, technology, culture, and related fields.

You might contact us about reading groups, seminars, co-writing, visiting talks, workshops, conferences, shared syllabi, collaborative projects, or simply to say that these questions resonate with your work.

Email: contact@example.org

A Gentle Note

This collective is imagined as open rather than membership-heavy. Participation can be occasional, project-based, local, online, experimental, slow, or still forming.

  • Share a short research interest or bio.
  • Suggest a text, workshop, seminar, or collaboration.
  • Tell us what more-than-digital futures mean in your context.
